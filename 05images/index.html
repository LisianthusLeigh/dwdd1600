Welcome, dear traveler, to the Feywild! Are you ready for magic, mystery, and –most importantly– fairies?

This DnD 5e module is written as a mystery/roleplay campaign for those interested in exploring the mysterious and whimsical plane known as the Feywild. Official 5e sources lack much depth in regards to the feywild, and so while much of this is inspired by 5e sources (such as Wild Beyond the Witchlight) it is almost entirely homebrew.

You start this story with a hag, known as Babushka Druadel. She has gathered all the player characters for a quest that will take them across all the seasonal courts of the feywild. She has summoned them… to gather her groceries. Players will venture first to the winter court, guided by an ever-shifting map and a magical list, to gather the mundane and magical items on this Hag’s Grocery List. Once they have completed the list of items in each court, they move onto the next, but the feywild is far more than initially meets the eye, and players will uncover a plot much larger –and far more sinister– than their quest.

"Do me a favor?"

Babushka Druadel is an Arch Hag, and an old one to boot, but she is not as sinister or cruel as most hags –at least, she doesn’t seem to be. She appears to be a kind old woman, and when she encounters someone who could use help, she offers them a deal: A favor for a favor. She does what it is that they wish for, and in exchange they promise to answer her call if she needs them.

Each player character in this campaign has made this very deal with Babushka, and now she has called on them to fulfill their end of the bargain.

The World

The feywild is a truly magnificent place. It is a mirror dimension to that of the Material Plane, but that does not mean it abides by all the rules of our world. Many who travel to these lands describe it as being dreamlike, surreal, impossible. No place stays in the same spot for long in these lands, and yet there are no days or nights to mark the passage of time. In fact, time does not seem to move the same there, and the seasons do not change. The feywild appears to be separated into demiplanes by most mortals, and these have earned the title of Fey Courts. There are 4 main courts of Seelie, those being the seasonal courts. This quest will take players through all 4 of these courts (and beyond).

About the Creator

Hi! I’m Leigh, and I’m the creator of this dnd module. I initially created it as a 4-shot for me and my friends to play but, in typical Dungeon Master fashion, I ended up going really deep into worldbuilding and creating a multi-year campaign! Because of how much I’ve built in this world, I figured I would share it with the world, in case anyone finds it useful or inspirational. A lot of the worldbuilding was influenced by my players but I have made efforts to leave space for you to customize the setting to your players. There are more details regarding that in the relevant places on this site, but I hope you enjoy this setting!
