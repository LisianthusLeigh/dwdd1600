Encounter Tables

Forest Encounters

D10 Encounter Title Encounter Description
1 Eladrin Encounter Eladrin are very common in the spring and summer courts, and in this encounter players witness some eladrin going about their daily, whimsical lives! Roll a d4 to see what event they witness
  1. Two eladrin passionately make out against a tree, if players stare too long, the eladrin will react hostile. In the unlikely event that there is a fight that starts, roll initiative and use the stats of a goblin or something.
  2. Three teenage eladrin are climbing trees and gossiping. Players can make a DC 16 perception check to hear what they are discussing (its something ridiculous like crushes or school drama, but with hints and references to other things)
  3. A lone eladrin sits at the edge of a pond, writing in their journal. Players must succeed a stealth check DC 9 or be noticed by them. The eladrin will be friendly but absentminded, thinking deeply about their work
  4. An older eladrin woman (still looks young) is foraging for berries and herbs for her stew! If players help her (costs 1 hour token), she will give each player a kiss on the cheek and they gain one d8 of inspiration that does not expire.
2 Sprite Illusion Players are shown an illusion of something fantastic that they would want. Roll a d6 and consult the table below to find what illusion. A DC 16 insight check will tell them that it is an illusion, if they choose to roll. If players approach the illusion, it will dissipate and all players within 10 feet of the illusion are doused with icy, cold water. Each player takes 1 point of cold damage from the water as sprites of many kinds laugh at their suffering. At any inclination of combat, the sprites will flee.
  1. A table filled with the players favorite foods, drinks, and treats. The table is unoccupied, and looks ready for the taking
  2. A young fairy calls out for help, she’s lost her beecat in the tree!
  3. A wounded fey deer lies in a field, licking its wounds. At its side is a young foal, its child, who whines in fear
  4. A sword, pulsing magic power, lies in a stone. It speaks to the players in their minds, promising great power if they were to grab it.
  5. Beautiful Eladrin dance in a circle, they beckon the players to join them. Very flirty!
  6. A diary sits atop a wooden log. The cover says “Addison’s secret journal, Do not touch!!!” in a child's handwriting. It looks lost.
3 TwiddleToad players stumble across a twiddletoad! All players within 30 feet of the twiddletoad must make a wisdom saving throw (DC 13) or be charmed by the twiddletoad and are compelled to pet it. Each time the player touches the twiddletoad in any way, it suffers one level of twiddle poisoning (consult stat block)
4 Mantrap Plant a large man-eating carnivorous plant is in the way. Players within 60 ft of the plant must make a DC 13 constitution saving throw or be poisoned. The effect ends if the plant is killed or if the player is beyond 60 ft of the plant. Consult the stat block for additional effects.
5 Moss Cub players come across a moss cub, which looks like a large kitten made of moss. The moss kitten will be very friendly to them, but if they play with the moss cub for too long, its mama, the tiger lily, will appear and fight them for messing with its baby. Consult Stat block.
6 Puck Moment the deeth shows up again! This time as a different creature than a fey deer. Roll a d6 and consult the table below to see what creature he appears as!
  1. Almiraj
  2. Blink Dog
  3. Displacer Beast
  4. Long Ferret
  5. Pigeon/Rat Griffon
  6. Snail with Legs
7 Treant Players come across a friendly treant. If they befriend the treant, it will take them to the next hex without the cost of an hour token.
8 Red Caps Players stumble across a mutilated corpse of a fey creature (roll a d6, 1-2 is a fey deer, 3-4 is a harengon, and 5-6 is a satyr), from the carnage, 1d4-1 (Minimum of one) Red Caps appear. The players can sneak by with a DC 12 stealth check, or otherwise are spotted and must roll for initiative.
9 Penneth Attack A band of sneaky harengon giggle in the trees as they throw acorns at the players, roll 3 d20s to hit each player, and count how many hits. Each player takes 1d4 bludgeoning damage and gains 1 lucky point (that expires at the next long rest) for every acorn that hits.
10 Dryad Whilst wandering the forests, the players encounter a dryad pruning her leaves (essentially brushing her hair) while singing to herself. She will be friendly to them if they approach and invites them to dine in her orchards.

Hill Encounters

D10 Encounter Title Encounter Description
1 Sphinx While passing through a particularly arid and dry part of the mountainous hills, the players encounter a sphinx! The sphinx looks young and is very colorful. The sphinx refuses to let them pass without answering its “riddle" but its riddles are extremely lax and outright ridiculous. If the players mess up one riddle, the sphinx will ask another. If they can’t get any of them, the sphinx will ask them for dating advice.
2 Feystag Shopkeep Make a perception check, DC 12 to see a small cart parked up against a wall. The cart is filled with all sorts of goods and players can feel a magical energy humming from it. If they approach, a chipper Feystag/Calygraunt will greet them. The feystag is a merchant who trades in magical items, and he is willing to trade! Consult the pinterest board and spreadsheet for details.
3 Korred Dance Players stumble upon a group of korreds dancing around a large moss-covered stone. DC 6 perception check to notice them. The korreds will not stop dancing but they might beckon the players to join. If the players join, they will watch as the moss-covered stone begins to crack open magically, revealing crystals inside. The players can take some of the loose crystals as thanks for helping.
4 Oread While walking through a mountainous area of the hills, players make a DC 10 perception check to sense the thumps of a far off creature. If they pass, they can follow the thumps and see in the distance a giant woman made of stone. If they speak with the woman she will offer to move them over the mountains quicker, as she is large enough to carry them. This will cut 1 hour off the travel time.
5 Giggle Spring Players encounter a small hot spring of warm, iridescent water. If they touch the water, they must roll a DC 14 constitution saving throw or be thrown into a fit of giggles. If a player drinks the water, they automatically fail this saving throw but gain 1d6 points of healing for each gulp. The effect wears off after 1d12 minutes or players can expend an hour token to remove it immediately.
6 Tricked Item Players encounter a small chest, about 2 feet wide, in the middle of the wilderness. It’s cracked open and a magical light spills from within. If the players choose to look inside the chest, there will be a small burst of light. Roll on the Tricked Item table to see what it does.
7 Lost Item Roll a perception check (DC 12) to spot this. On the path in front of the players, there is a small item that has clearly been lost. Roll a d6 to see what they find
  1. A small stuffed brushwagg plush, with yarn brambles. It is clearly well loved and sized for a child. (this belongs to the nonbinary Penneth in the magnolia clan)
  2. A beautiful necklace with a glowing stone in the center (a wish stone) and engraved on the back are the initials SF (this belongs to Siri the Satyr, she lost it on her way to the winter court)
  3. A shiny brass key, decorative more than useful, and with an odd magical aura. (Belongs to puck, purposefully lost. If whoever finds it keeps it, they have disadvantage against puck until they get rid of it)
  4. A blanket embroidered with candystems. The fabric is soft but dirty from lying around. It isn’t very big, but could easily be a blanket for a brownie or gnome (this blanket belongs to Takeo, as he loves candystems)
  5. A golden cup, embellished with jewels and engravings. It is very old and tarnished, but keeps a certain shine to it. There is no magic aura, and it even seems too normal. (This cup belongs/belonged to the Goose)
  6. A small bucket, like literally so small. It is the size of a bucket that a mouse would use! (this belongs to the brigganock Tim, as it is sized for him!)
8 Owlbear Cub Players make a DC 11 perception check to notice the small owlbear cub bounding along the path in front of them. The cub will be shocked and suspicious but won’t run away. An animal handling check with a DC of 13 is required to gain the animals trust, else it will just run away. Succeeding the throw means that the owlbear will cuddle up and play with the players. If players spend too long with the owlbear or attempt to take it far from the grotto they found it in, there will be an angry owlbear mama who comes to attack them!
9 Puck Moment The trees have started to change, the players are now in the midst of a deep aspen forest. The eyes in the bark stare into the players minds and they suddenly feel as though they are being watched. Players must make an intelligence saving throw or become frightened as they are shown glimpses of their greatest fears (those who fail are not only afraid, but also have their minds read to understand their greatest fears).
10 Train Spirit Players encounter what looks like a rusted, beat up magi-tech train in the middle of their path. If they enter the train and roll a DC 16 investigation check, they will discover a skeleton holding a lantern. If they light the lantern, the skeleton will come to life and offer them a ride to anywhere in the court they want

Field Encounters

D10 Encounter Title Encounter Description
1 Flower Sprite Players walk through a field of various flowers, when suddenly they hear a tiny voice shout “Hey!” they can make a DC 16 perception check to find the flower sprite who is staring at them angrily. She explains that the players are walking through her territory and demands a toll be paid. This toll can be paid in food, trinkets, or similar. If players refuse, she will call for 1d6+2 other sprites and they will fight. Consult the stat block for more.
2 Fey Deer Herd Players spot a feydeer in the distance. A DC 10 perception check will show them the rest of the herd not far off. If players approach the fey deer, they must make a stealth check (DC 13) to not scare off the deer. If they succeed, they can get close to the deer, and make an animal handling check (DC 16) for the deer to trust them.
3 Runalongs players make a perception check, DC 12, to spot runalongs moving alongside them. If they slow down, the creatures will disappear. Any mount that runs alongside the runalongs gets a temporary +10 to movement speed until the next long rest.
4 Magic Mists Players encounter a land enshrouded in a dusty pink mist. Players have two choices, they can go around it and add 1d4 hours to their travel time or walk through it. If they walk through the mist, each player needs to make a constitution saving throw (DC 13) to resist the magic of the mists. On a failed save, the player will roll a d100 and the DM will consult the two curse tables to give the player a temporary curse.
  • Regular Curse Table
  • Fey Bargain Table
5 Puck Moment Puck once more shows up to stalk the players, but this time there will be a creature associated with druadel (roll a d4, 1 - Crow, 2- Rat, 3 - Tarantula, 4 - Boggle) that chases the creature off, so he is unable to truly observe them.
6 Dandelore A field of white dandelores stretch out in front of the players. They look like the white fluffy form of dandelions, but their seeds are white instead of black. If players pass through the field, their movements will jostle the seeds and each will receive a vision of a forgotten memory from the dandelore. Roll a d12 and consult the table for what memory they see.
  1. A memory of one of the players childhood, long forgotten with simply time
  2. A memory of Meliae, when she was alone
  3. a memory of Puck doing something evil as Cuvindo
  4. a memory of a random harengon kid playing chase with their litter siblings
  5. a memory of someone paying tribute to a god that is no longer known
  6. a memory of an eladrin girl coming home proud of her art project from school
  7. a memory of a middle aged human chastising a remorseless cat for breaking her vase
  8. a memory of a cobalt dragonborn man furiously trying to write romantic poetry for his beloved
  9. a memory of a small half-elf boy playing with a small and worn stuffed sheep
  10. a memory of a small tabaxi child weeping in the forest alone, they are not found or comforted
  11. a memory of a dwarf child learning to cook a basic stew
  12. a memory of an older halfling woman being insulted by several delinquent elves
7 Knights of Chitter & Chatter “HALT!” cries a voice, it is a knight of Chitter and Chatter! [make a perception check, DC 14 to see the knight] The knight tells the players to go no further in this direction, as there is a dangerous patch of Obliviax and that the knights of Chitter and Chatter are taking care of it! Players are given a detour that adds only 1 hour of travel time. If players wish to encounter the moss, they must first succeed a DC 18 persuasion check or DC 16 stealth check against the Knight of Chitter and Chatter.
8 Almiraj Stampede No perception check necessary, the players hear a rumbling and the increasing pitter patter of paws. All players must make a dexterity saving throw (DC 15) to avoid being trampled by the almiraj stampede! If they fail the saving throw they take 1d4 bludgeoning damage and 1d4 piercing damage (from their horns)
9 Hybsil While moving, players will spot small creatures running through the fields. They will cut in front of the players, moving too fast to be spoken to, but if players succeed a history check they get to know what kinds of creatures they are!
10 Feldgeisters Players stumble upon a feylost farm, the house long abandoned and the fields overgrown. Within these fields are the mischevious but neutral wind spirits known as Feldgeisters. They will attempt to race the players and trip them, but will do nothing more harmful than laugh at their clumsiness
